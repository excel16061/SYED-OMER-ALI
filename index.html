Current research

The use of digital twins in predictive maintenance of computer-controlled systems

Graduate research · Avila University Digital twins · Predictive maintenance · Cybersecurity · IoT

This research examines how digital twins, virtual representations of a physical system that are updated with real-time data, can support predictive maintenance of computer-controlled systems. The goal is to identify failures before they cause unexpected downtime, and to understand the technical and organizational challenges of implementing these systems.

  • Reviewing how digital twins are integrated with predictive maintenance and machine learning models.
  • Comparing the technologies most effective for real-time monitoring and early fault detection.
  • Identifying implementation challenges data complexity, computational requirements, interoperability, and scalability.
  • Evaluating how digital twin-based maintenance affects downtime, maintenance costs, reliability, and performance.

Next: extending selective fine-tuning toward continual learning, so the adapted model retains earlier activities as it learns new ones.
